Our story

The name metoo. comes from the Thai word "มีตู้" (mee-tûu), meaning "to have a shelf." That's our whole concept — finding shelf space for local brands and connecting them with retailers who can carry them. Read the other way, in English it becomes "me too!" — the feeling every small brand deserves: a seat at the table, a place on the shelf, and a community that says you belong here, too.
